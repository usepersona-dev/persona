## Collection and use of information

When you use or access the Services, we collect certain categories of information about you from a variety of sources.

Besides the specific uses described below, we may use the information we collect to provide, develop and improve the Services. That includes understanding your requests, planning and completing tasks for you (which may involve engaging third parties for you), generating responses, personalizing suggestions based on the context of your conversations and your past use of the Services, and improving and redesigning features across the Services. We also use it to communicate with you, measure and analyze performance, keep the Services safe and secure, comply with our legal obligations, and protect our rights and the rights of our employees and others. We do not sell your information, and we do not disclose it to registered data brokers or to third parties who resell it.

### Information you give us directly

Some features of the Services require you to give us information about yourself. You can choose not to, but you may then be unable to use those features. Information you give us directly includes:

- **Contact and account details,** such as your name, business name, address, phone number, email address and country. We use them to create and maintain your account, provide the Services, and communicate with you, including about products or services that may interest you. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider.
- **Payment information,** such as card details and billing address, and for Partners, payout information. Our third-party payment processor collects it. We use it to process payments and payouts and to provide the Services. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Agent configuration,** if you are a Partner: the instructions, skills, knowledge, documents, brand voice, pricing, integrations, automations, webhooks, MCP servers and API keys you set up for an agent.
- **Anything else you include when you communicate with us or with an agent,** for example a message you send an agent on WhatsApp or Telegram. We use it to provide the Services.

### Information collected through your use of the Services

Agents think, plan and act to help with everyday tasks. To do that, an agent is available whenever someone engages it, and, depending on the permissions granted, it may access the information shared with or made available to it. That includes:

- **Messaging identifiers:** on WhatsApp, your phone number, display name and profile name; on Telegram, your user ID, username and display name; and message metadata such as timestamps and delivery status.
- **Conversation content:** messages, voice notes and other audio, images, documents and links sent to the agent, and the agent's replies.
- **Connected services:** information from apps and services you connect, such as a calendar, email account or delivery service, including the content of messages, emails and other private communications or materials you make available to the agent, and the access tokens for those services.
- **Credentials:** usernames, passwords, API keys and other secrets added to an agent's Vault so it can sign in to or use a service. They are stored encrypted. The AI model receives their names, not their values, and the values are passed only to the browser or code environment a task needs.
- **Browsing:** when an agent uses a web browser for you, the pages it visits and what it types and submits there. Signed-in browser sessions may be kept so the agent stays signed in later.
- **Memory:** facts and preferences an agent saves so it can help you later, such as a delivery address or a dietary preference.

Depending on the permissions you grant and how you use an agent, it may have access to information that is considered sensitive. For example, you might give an agent your payment details so it can place an order for you, or the username and password for a third-party account so it can sign in for you. You might also use an agent to handle health-related information, for example to book a medical appointment or summarize an email from your doctor. And, as noted above, if you connect your accounts, the agent can access the content of the messages, emails and other private communications you make available to it.

As described above, we use information collected through your use of the Services to develop and improve our products and technology, including to evaluate, fine-tune and train the AI models that power them. If you do not want us to use your information to train AI models, you can opt out by emailing info@usepersona.dev. Even if you opt out, we may still use your information for model training when it is flagged for safety review, to improve how we detect harmful content, enforce our policies or advance our safety research. The opt-out takes effect going forward and covers only model training, not other improvements to the Services. Opting out may make the Services less suited to your particular use. We may keep using AI models that were trained, fine-tuned or improved with your information before you opted out. We never use credentials stored in a Vault, or information received from Google Workspace (see below), to evaluate, fine-tune, train or improve AI models.

The Services and their agents are not professional advisors. Do not rely on them for actions that need financial, legal, medical or other professional advice or judgment.

## Data security

We protect information with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), encrypted storage of connected-service tokens and Vault credentials, limited and multi-factor-protected access to production data, logical separation of each Partner's data and agents, and logging, monitoring and a documented incident response process. We do not currently hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. Despite these efforts, no security measure is impenetrable, and we cannot guarantee perfect security. If we learn of a breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected Partners without undue delay, and within 72 hours, and notify authorities and individuals where the law requires.

AI agents can raise extra privacy and security considerations, because they can carry out tasks and take actions on their own, within the permissions you grant. As with any technology that acts for you, there are risks to keep in mind. An agent may take unintended actions (including unintended payments or messages to other people), produce wrong or incomplete output, or meet third-party content containing misleading instructions written to influence AI agents (sometimes called prompt injection). We encourage you to review what agents do, stay attentive while using the Services, and keep these risks in mind. Give an agent only the access it needs, and disconnect services and remove credentials it no longer needs.

**WhatsApp.** Messages travel over WhatsApp, which uses end-to-end encryption between users and WhatsApp's business infrastructure. Because the agent has to read messages to reply, they are decrypted when they reach our systems and our messaging provider. Meta handles message data under its own policies.

**Telegram.** Chats with Telegram bots are not end-to-end encrypted. Telegram handles message data under its own policies.

Information you send us electronically may not be secure in transit. We recommend that you do not use unsecured channels to send us sensitive or confidential information.
