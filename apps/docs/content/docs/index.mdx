Persona is the agent factory. You make AI agents there, one or a few hundred, for your business, your family or your customers. In Persona, an agent is called a **persona**: who it is, the rules it follows, the skills it knows and the apps it can use.

People reach a persona where they already talk: WhatsApp, Telegram or the web. Slack and iMessage are coming soon. The persona answers in plain text, like a person texting. It can also text first, on a schedule.

## What a persona can do

- **Follow rules.** Instructions are named rules that the persona follows in every conversation. Each rule switches on and off by itself.
- **Use skills.** A skill is a procedure in Markdown, with its files. Write one, or install one from [skills.sh](https://skills.sh).
- **Use apps.** Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub and hundreds more connect through [Composio](https://composio.dev). Any MCP server connects by its URL.
- **Run on a schedule.** An automation is a task the persona does on its own, once or again and again. It reports the result to the chat it came from.
- **React to other apps.** A webhook is a secret address that a form, a store or Zapier calls. Each call runs a task, and the result can go to a chat. A trigger does the same for an event in a connected app, such as a new email.
- **Keep secrets safe.** The vault holds logins, cards and API keys, sealed in the database. The person types them on a form, never in the chat.
- **Work in a browser.** The persona can open a real browser, sign in from the vault and do a task on a website.
- **Make and read media.** It draws and edits images, films short videos, speaks text out loud and reads photos, PDFs and recordings.
- **Remember.** Each persona has its own memory of the people it talks to.
- **Keep notes.** The persona writes notes for itself, such as lists and drafts, and finds them again.

## How it fits together

An **organization** owns **projects**. A project owns **personas**.

```
organization
└── project
    └── persona   (models, instructions, skills, MCP servers, apps, vault, files, notes, automations, webhooks, triggers, chats)
```

The persona is the unit. Everything hangs off a persona: its setup, its memory, its files and its automations. When two people need privacy from each other, each gets a persona of their own.

## Built on one API

The app is a client of the [REST API](/api). Most of what the app does, you can do over HTTP with an API key; notes and triggers are the persona's own, through its tools. The persona can also change most of its own setup in a conversation: its tools write the same rows as the API.
